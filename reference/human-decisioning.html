Reference architecture

Put people where consequence lives

Human work is part of the architecture, but it should appear because judgement, authority or customer need requires it, not because a department historically owns a stage.

Human work is a decision service

The target is not “human in the loop everywhere”. It is also not “human out of the loop”. Human involvement should be explicit, reasoned and bounded to the decisions where accountability adds value or is required.

TriggerWhy a person is involvedDecision pack
Adverse or rights-affecting outcomeCoverage denial/partial denial, material liability/adverse settlement position or other outcome where the control model requires accountable review.Decision basis, evidence, policy/rule references, uncertainty, proposed outcome, customer context and required authority.
Material ambiguityEvidence is conflicting or policy interpretation is not sufficiently clear for bounded automated handling.Conflicting evidence, ambiguity statement, available alternatives and impact of each interpretation.
Vulnerability / supportCustomer circumstances materially affect communication, support or appropriateness of action.Relevant support context only. Minimise sensitive information exposure.
High consequence / irreversibilityFinancial, legal, safety or customer impact exceeds the automation boundary.Consequence assessment, authority, reversibility and proposed controls.
InvestigationMaterial integrity concern meets controlled criteria for investigation or investigation scope changes.Concern, evidence, alternatives, proportionality, scope and review point.
Control exceptionA deterministic control blocks normal flow but an authorised exception path exists.Control failure, exception basis, delegated authority and compensating controls.

Human task contract

  • State exactly why the human is required.
  • Present the decision question, not an undifferentiated claim file.
  • Show the evidence and provenance that materially supports the decision.
  • Show model/rule recommendations separately from facts.
  • Expose material unknowns and conflicting evidence.
  • Show delegated authority and controls that apply.
  • Capture rationale, override and outcome as structured data.
  • Do not block unrelated eligible actions while the task is open.

No fake human oversight

Design warning

A human clicking “approve” on a machine-generated answer they cannot meaningfully interrogate is not strong human accountability. The task has to give the reviewer enough evidence, context and time to make an independent decision.
