Reference architecture

Bound the intelligence

The design uses specialised services with explicit inputs, outputs and prohibitions. The point is not more agents. The point is clearer responsibility.

Service catalogue

IDServiceMechanismResponsibilityBoundary
INT-01Notification structuring serviceAI / document intelligenceExtract entities, facts, attachments and unknowns from incoming content.Cannot silently overwrite source evidence or infer missing facts as truth.
INT-02Coverage intelligence serviceAI / rules / retrievalAssemble relevant policy clauses, facts and evidence into a cited coverage finding.Finding is not final coverage authority unless separately permitted by the decision/control model.
INT-03Damage and quantum serviceVision / analytics / rulesAssess damage evidence, repairability, scope hypothesis, quantum range and assessment need.Must expose uncertainty and escalate when physical/expert assessment could materially change outcome.
INT-04Liability intelligence serviceAI / rulesDevelop liability position, evidence gaps and recovery implications.Unresolved liability should not automatically block unrelated first-party actions.
INT-05Integrity intelligence serviceAnalytics / AIDetect inconsistencies/anomalies and recommend proportionate next evidence.Anomaly is not fraud and cannot independently trigger intrusive investigation.
INT-06Customer context serviceAI / rules / humanMaintain communication/support context from interactions and explicit customer needs.Minimise sensitive data and do not infer protected/sensitive attributes beyond permitted use.
INT-07Recovery intelligence serviceRules / analyticsIdentify early recovery opportunities and evidence-preservation needs.Recovery work does not own the customer claim.
INT-08Claim economics serviceAnalyticsCompare expected indemnity, handling cost, evidence cost, delay cost and recovery value.Economics cannot override rights, policy terms or mandatory controls.
DEC-01Evidence sufficiency serviceRules + bounded reasoningAssess whether a material unknown remains for a specific action.Cannot treat model confidence as legal/financial authority.
DEC-02Authority and control serviceDeterministic rulesApply delegated authority, regulatory/policy controls and hard stop conditions.No free-form AI override.
HUM-01Human decision serviceHuman taskingRoute a decision-ready pack and capture structured rationale/authority/override.Must not become a generic work queue.
ACT-01Action execution servicesAPIs / automation / robotsExecute payments, appointments, repair instructions, messages and system updates.Requires idempotency, reconciliation and explicit error paths.
COM-01Customer communication serviceTemplates / rules / AI drafting + controlsGenerate and deliver contextual communications and capture delivery/response events.Generated text must not outrun the underlying approved decision or omit required disclosures.
CTL-01Obligation/timer serviceRules / timersCreate, monitor and escalate Code, complaint, supplier, investigation and internal timers.Must support exceptions/alternative timetables where the underlying obligation permits them.

Do not build one “Claims Agent”

A single agent that reads the entire claim, decides the outcome, triggers payment and writes the customer letter is easy to demo and difficult to govern. It collapses reasoning, authority, execution and evidence into one opaque boundary.

Specialise the reasoning. Centralise the state. Make controls deterministic. Keep execution bounded.
