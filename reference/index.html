Reference architecture

Separate reasoning from authority and execution

The architecture is deliberately modular. No single component is allowed to reason, authorise, execute and record a consequential claim outcome without independent controls.

Separation of responsibility

ResponsibilityPrimary mechanismWhat it should not become
Reasoning / interpretationBounded AI or analytic serviceAuthority to execute an adverse or consequential action by itself
Deterministic policy and controlRules / DMN / codeFree-form LLM judgement
Long-running coordinationProcess / case orchestrationA single “super agent” trying to remember the whole claim
System actionAPI / automation / robotHidden reasoning or uncontrolled side effects
Accountable judgementHuman decision serviceRoutine queue work that exists only because of historical ownership
Record / evidenceSystems of record and Claim StateA chat transcript used as the source of truth

Architecture rule

Agents advise and interpret. Orchestration governs. Rules constrain. Services act. Humans own consequential ambiguity.
