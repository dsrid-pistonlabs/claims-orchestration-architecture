Implementation

Test the operating model, not just the model

A good LLM evaluation does not prove the claims process works. Production assurance has to cover process, controls, humans, integrations, side effects, resilience and customer outcomes together.

Assurance model

Assurance areaMinimum expectation
Process conformanceEvery BPMN path, event, timer, exception and closure route has an executable test case.
Decision correctnessGolden claim sets cover expected, ambiguous, adverse and boundary outcomes with documented expected basis.
Evidence provenanceEvery material finding/decision can be traced back to source evidence and versions.
Control testingEach hard control has positive, negative, boundary and exception-path tests.
Human decision qualityDecision packs are reviewed for completeness, biasing presentation, authority and rationale quality.
AI evaluationMeasure extraction/reasoning by task and claim type, not one generic “accuracy” score. Include abstention and unsupported-claim rates.
Side-effect safetyDuplicate, timeout, retry, partial failure and reconciliation scenarios are explicitly tested.
ResilienceLoss of model, API, supplier, data source or orchestration component has a safe degraded path and recovery test.
Privacy/securityAccess, data minimisation, logging, retention, test-data controls and incident scenarios are tested in the entity framework.
Customer outcomeMonitor delay, unnecessary evidence requests, complaint signals, repeat contact, cash-settlement outcomes and vulnerability/support outcomes where applicable.

Go-live gates

  • Process paths and timeouts are tested, including exception and alternative-timetable paths.
  • Decision/control rules are versioned and independently reviewed.
  • Human roles, authorities and escalation paths are operationally staffed.
  • Fallback mode works when AI or external data is unavailable.
  • Side-effecting actions reconcile against systems of record.
  • Audit reconstruction has been demonstrated on representative claims.
  • Customer communications have legal/compliance/content-owner approval where required.
  • Operational risk, information security, privacy and service-provider requirements are signed off through the insurer’s normal governance.
