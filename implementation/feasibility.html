Implementation

This can be built now

The architecture deliberately avoids capabilities that depend on speculative autonomy. It uses mature orchestration, rules, data, integration and human-work patterns, with AI bounded to tasks where it can be measured.

Practical feasibility

No part of the core operating model requires a hypothetical future technology. Some intelligence domains need product-specific validation, but the architecture can be built now.

CapabilityPositionTypical implementation optionsPractical caveat
Persistent Claim StateBuild nowCase/state store, event log, relational/document databaseData model, versioning and integration discipline are the hard parts, not technology availability.
Long-running orchestrationBuild nowBPMN/case orchestration, workflow engineMust support waits, timers, events, human tasks, retries and correlation without holding synchronous sessions.
Deterministic controlsBuild nowRules engine, DMN, policy-as-code or governed servicesKeep externally-derived requirements and delegated authorities versioned and testable.
Document / email structuringBuild now with validationDocument intelligence + LLM extractionPreserve original evidence and measure field-level accuracy by document type.
Coverage intelligenceBuild now, boundedRetrieval + rules + LLM reasoningRequires authoritative wording/version retrieval, citation and human/control boundary for consequential outcomes.
Damage / quantum intelligenceBuild by claim typeVision, estimating data, rules, expert serviceEvidence quality and calibration vary materially by peril/asset. Physical/expert fallback remains essential.
Liability intelligenceBuild by defined scenarioRules + evidence reasoningLegal/factual ambiguity requires strong human decision paths and scenario-specific validation.
Integrity intelligenceBuild as decision supportAnalytics + anomaly detection + reasoningAnomaly cannot equal fraud. Investigation must remain proportionate and controlled.
Customer context / communicationBuild now with privacy controlsInteraction analytics, templates, LLM draftingAvoid inferring sensitive characteristics. Generated communications cannot outrun approved decisions.
Payments / supplier instructionsBuild now with hard controlsAPIs/RPA + reconciliationSide effects need idempotency, authority, error handling and reconciliation.
Human decision serviceBuild nowTask/case managementQuality depends on the decision pack and structured rationale, not the task UI.
Audit / observabilityBuild from day oneEvent logs, telemetry, lineage storeRetrofitting decision provenance after go-live is expensive and usually incomplete.

What should be piloted first

Choose a claim archetype where the decision boundary is understood, data quality is measurable and at least one customer-beneficial action can be released before the whole claim is complete. Prove the orchestration pattern before trying to cover every product and peril.

Pilot success is not “the agent answered correctly”

Success is that the claim state stayed coherent, controls were respected, evidence was traceable, humans were invoked for the right reasons, actions were safe and the customer outcome improved.
