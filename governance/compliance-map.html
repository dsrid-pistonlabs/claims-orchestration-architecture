Scope caveats

  • The General Insurance Code is an industry code, not legislation. Applicability depends on insurer subscription, product and Code scope.
  • Insurance Contracts Act applicability has statutory exceptions and product-specific considerations. Do not reduce it to a generic “claims law” rule.
  • APRA standards apply to APRA-regulated entities and must be implemented through the entity’s broader prudential framework.
  • Product, state/territory, statutory scheme, third-party claim, workers compensation, CTP and other specialised contexts can introduce different requirements.
  • The architecture must be validated against the actual policy wording, delegated authorities, licence conditions and internal risk appetite before production.
