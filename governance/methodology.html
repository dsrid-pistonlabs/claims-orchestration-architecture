Method & evidence

Designed to be challenged, not admired

The publication separates external obligations, future obligations, design choices and implementation examples so strong architecture statements remain defensible.

Evidence classification

ClassMeaningHow it is used
LAW / REGLegislation or enforceable regulatory requirement/guidance context.Architecture response must be validated against the exact legal/entity/product circumstances.
CODEGeneral Insurance Code of Practice standard.Relevant for subscribing insurers and in-scope products. It is not presented as legislation.
PRUDENTIALAPRA prudential standard.Relevant to APRA-regulated entities and implemented through the entity’s prudential framework.
INCOMINGKnown requirement with a future commencement date.Designed for now but labelled as not yet in force as at the publication date.
DESIGNReference-architecture choice.Defensible design control or pattern, not an external legal requirement.
ILLUSTRATIVEExample only.Used to make implementation concrete without implying it is mandatory.

Scope assumptions

  • Primary context is Australian general insurance claims handled by an insurer or claims-handling service within the relevant financial-services framework.
  • The publication is product-agnostic. It does not encode one insurer’s policy wording, delegated authorities, systems or risk appetite.
  • It is not legal advice, compliance certification or a substitute for product/jurisdiction-specific review.
  • It deliberately separates architecture that is broadly reusable from controls that depend on insurer, product, Code, prudential and legal scope.
  • Public authoritative sources are preferred over secondary summaries for regulatory statements.

Practitioner position

The recurring test is practical: what genuinely blocks the customer outcome, what evidence actually changes the decision, what can run independently, where must a human own consequence, and how does the organisation prove what happened later?

That is why the site does not lead with “AI agents”. The technology matters after the operating model, decision boundary and control model are clear.
