Process architecture

4.0 / 7.0 Outcome Execution and Closure

Execute only actions that have passed readiness and controls. Publish outcomes back to Claim State, then close the customer-facing claim only when the outcome and mandatory obligations are complete.

Process view

Required behaviour

  • Use unique action IDs and idempotency for payment, supplier instruction, appointments and notifications.
  • External providers return milestone, delay, quality and exception events.
  • Recovery, salvage, litigation or reconciliation may continue as linked residual work after customer closure.
  • Closure is a decision, not the absence of open tasks.
  • Every execution result updates Claim State and can trigger new readiness evaluation.

Relevant controls

These are the controls most directly connected to this process area. Applicability still depends on product, Code subscription, licence, jurisdiction and entity context.

CODE CTRL-03

External appointment communication

Notify specified assessor/adjuster/investigator appointments within 5 business days, control expert timeframes/qualification and communicate expert delay where required.

Basis: GICOP 72-75

CODE CTRL-06

Urgent financial need

Fast-track assessment/decision and/or advance-payment controls apply where the paragraph 64 circumstances are met.

Basis: GICOP 64-66

CODE CTRL-08

Service supplier accountability

Supplier use does not remove insurer accountability; appointment, subcontracting, conduct, complaints and authorised-repair quality controls remain with the insurer.

Basis: GICOP 35-41, 86-87

LAW CTRL-09

AFS claims handling conduct

Claims handling is a regulated financial service. The operating model must support efficient, honest and fair service, competent representatives, supervision and disclosure obligations.

Basis: Corporations Act s912A; ASIC INFO 253

LAW/ASIC CTRL-11

Cash settlement disclosure and fairness

Where a Cash Settlement Fact Sheet is legally required, issue it at the offer point with prescribed content. Settlement design must also support realistic, transparent amounts and informed customer choice.

Basis: INFO 253; ASIC 2026 cash-settlement review

DESIGN CTRL-15

Action idempotency

Side-effecting actions such as payments, repair instructions, appointments and notifications require unique action IDs, duplicate prevention, retries, reconciliation and exception handling.

Basis: Blueprint control
