Process architecture

2.0 Claim Intelligence Management

Develop multiple evidence views at the same time, but only when the domain is relevant to the claim. No domain waits for all the others to complete.

Process view

Required behaviour

  • Coverage, damage/quantum, liability, integrity, customer context, recovery and economics are independent publishers.
  • Each finding carries evidence references, reliability/confidence, material unknowns and action implications.
  • A finding is not automatically a decision.
  • Slow liability, recovery or integrity work must not block unrelated customer-beneficial action.
  • There is no global join. New evidence can re-open or revise a finding.

2.3
Develop Liability Intelligence		Liability relevant and circumstances/evidence availableStatements, scene/event facts, applicable rules, third-party evidenceLiability position, confidence, evidence gap, recovery implicationsUnresolved liability must not automatically block first-party repair/support where the proposed action does not depend on it.
2.4
Develop Integrity Intelligence		Claim State/evidence changes or integrity signal receivedCross-source consistency, claim/policy history, evidence metadata, approved fraud indicatorsIntegrity concern, materiality, rationale, next evidence recommendationAn anomaly is not fraud. Escalation must be based on materiality and controlled investigation criteria.
2.5
Develop Customer Context Intelligence		Customer interaction or context changesCustomer statements, communication needs, vulnerability/hardship indicators, representation/support needsCustomer support profile, preferred communication, required accommodations and routingTreat support needs as dynamic. Customer context can change the process without changing the claim facts.
2.6
Develop Recovery Intelligence		Third-party/recovery facts emergeLiability/recovery facts, third-party details, salvage/subrogation evidenceRecovery opportunity, evidence preservation actions, expected valueRecovery can start early and continue after customer outcome. It should rarely hold the customer claim hostage.
2.7
Develop Claim Economics		Material cost/uncertainty/action changesExpected indemnity, handling cost, assessment/investigation cost, delay cost, recovery potentialExpected-value comparison and evidence/action economicsEconomics informs proportionality. It must not override rights, policy terms or mandatory controls.

Relevant controls

These are the controls most directly connected to this process area. Applicability still depends on product, Code subscription, licence, jurisdiction and entity context.

CODE CTRL-07

Vulnerability support

Customer vulnerability is a dynamic process control affecting communication, support, representation, identification and interpreter handling.

Basis: GICOP 91-103

LAW CTRL-09

AFS claims handling conduct

Claims handling is a regulated financial service. The operating model must support efficient, honest and fair service, competent representatives, supervision and disclosure obligations.

Basis: Corporations Act s912A; ASIC INFO 253
