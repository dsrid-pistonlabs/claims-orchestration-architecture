Process architecture

One claim, multiple concurrent lifecycles

The L1 BPMN is intentionally readable. It shows the operating model, not every rule and integration detail.

L1 collaboration

The important design choice is what is not present: there is no gateway that waits for every intelligence domain to finish before the claim may progress. The decision-readiness loop responds to material Claim State changes and evaluates candidate actions independently.

How to read the master

ElementInterpretation
Claim StateThe durable coordination object. It holds versioned facts, evidence, findings, unknowns, actions, obligations and context.
Parallel activationStarts long-running lifecycle capabilities. It is not a fan-out/fan-in batch job.
Material signalAny event that changes the decision basis or action eligibility can trigger re-evaluation.
Human decisioningA separate service invoked for consequence, ambiguity, authority or controlled exceptions.
FulfilmentSide-effecting actions publish success, failure and exception events back to Claim State.
ClosureAssessed explicitly. Residual work can transition without keeping the customer-facing claim open.
