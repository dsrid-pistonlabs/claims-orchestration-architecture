Process architecture

The process becomes buildable at L3

A process diagram tells you where work sits. A function contract tells you what must happen, what it needs and what it is allowed to produce.

L3 function register

Every function is written as a contract. The trigger and output should be testable. The implementation note captures the design intent that must survive vendor or organisational changes.
