Operating model

The claim stays still. The work moves.

Parallelism is not the goal by itself. The goal is to stop making the customer wait for dependencies that do not actually affect the outcome being considered.

What remains sequential

DependencyWhy sequence remains
Claim notification before establishmentA claim/case identity and initial context must exist before most governed activity can be attached and audited.
Relevant evidence before a decision that depends on itThe redesign identifies the material gap precisely. It does not remove genuine evidence requirements.
Authority/control before consequential executionA recommendation, model score or employee intention is not execution authority.
Valid decision before final decision communicationDrafting can start early. Release cannot pre-date the decision it communicates.
Customer outcome and mandatory obligations before closureSeparable residual work can transition, but the customer-facing claim cannot close simply because the queue is empty.

What runs continuously

CapabilityContinuous behaviour
Claim intelligenceEach domain re-evaluates when relevant evidence changes. Findings publish independently.
Customer communicationAcknowledgement, progress, requests, appointments, decisions, vulnerability support and complaint recognition respond to events and timers.
ControlsCode, licence, privacy, authority, supplier, complaint, investigation and audit controls can act at any point.
ReadinessEvery material Claim State update can make one action eligible while leaving another blocked.
